COMECIL (Computer, Mechanical & Civil Engineering Solutions) is a specialized multidisciplinary engineering practice uniting software developers, mechanical designers, and structural engineers.

Who We Are

Your Trusted Engineering Partner

We bridge the gap between academic theory, digital innovation, and real-world industrial practice.

At COMECIL, every project is approached with meticulous attention to detail, strict academic integrity, and an unwavering commitment to client satisfaction. Founded to provide comprehensive, cross-disciplinary technical support, we collaborate with university researchers, postgraduate students, industrial contractors, engineering consultants, and tech startups across the globe.

Whether designing an enterprise software ecosystem, conducting finite element simulation on an automotive component, or modeling a 15-storey building subjected to dynamic seismic forces, our multidisciplinary structure ensures that each challenge is tackled with specialized expertise.

Our Departments

Multidisciplinary Engineering Pillars

Three distinct departments working together under one synchronized engineering umbrella.

Department 01

Computer Science & IT

Our software division develops reliable, secure, and modern digital applications. We specialize in full-stack web platforms, mobile application development, database management systems, AI algorithms, neural network computer vision, and workflow automation.

  • • Modern Web Platforms (React, Node.js, PHP, Python)
  • • Deep Learning & Machine Learning Models
  • • Scalable Relational & NoSQL Database Systems
  • • Capstone & Academic Project Mentorship
Department 02

Mechanical Engineering

Our mechanical engineering group converts concepts into functional, manufacture-ready designs. We utilize industry-grade CAD software, finite element structural stress analysis (FEA), computational fluid dynamics (CFD), and thermodynamic simulations.

  • • SolidWorks 3D Part & Assembly Modeling
  • • ANSYS Mechanical FEA & Fatigue Analysis
  • • ANSYS Fluent CFD & Heat Transfer Simulations
  • • GD&T Production Blueprints & Manufacturing Plans
Department 03

Civil Engineering

Our civil infrastructure and structural analysis team delivers building designs, structural computations, and estimation services. We handle reinforced concrete, structural steel, foundation engineering, and quantity survey documentation.

  • • ETABS Multi-Storey Building Seismic Analysis
  • • SAFE Foundation & Raft Slab Design
  • • Architectural & Structural AutoCAD Drawings
  • • Detailed Bill of Quantities (BOQ) & Cost Estimation
