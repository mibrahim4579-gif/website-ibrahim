Our Process — How It Works

A transparent, four-stage workflow engineered to ensure precision, punctuality, and complete confidentiality from inquiry to delivery.

Step-by-Step Flow

From Concept to Final Handover

Structured stages with proactive communication every step of the way.

Stage 01

Submit Your Order

Fill out our structured order form with your project scope, engineering discipline, and specific service required. Attach relevant CAD drawings, research papers, or specification documents (PDF, DOCX, etc.).

Time: 2–5 Minutes
Stage 02

Requirements Review & Quote

Our senior engineering leads analyze your requirements, check technical feasibility, verify software tooling, and contact you within 24 hours with a confirmed timeline and milestone quote.

Response: ≤ 24 Hours
Stage 03

Engineering & Development

Dedicated domain experts execute your models, calculations, or code. Regular progress checkpoints allow you to review intermediate drafts, request fine-tuning, and ensure full alignment.

Checkpoints: Milestone Based
Stage 04

Quality Assurance & Delivery

Deliverables undergo strict QA verification against academic or industry standards. You receive full source code, parametric CAD models, simulation data, and comprehensive reports, backed by free revisions.

Includes: Free Revisions
